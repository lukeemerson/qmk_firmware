Neovim note lighting – decision doc

Your Air75 V2 while you take notes in Neovim. Neovim tells the board its mode, vault and note type over the USB cable. Everything below runs live, using the same lighting math as the firmware. The top half is what's decided. The bottom half is seven open decisions, each with options A, B and C, so you can reply in chat like D1 B, D2 A, ….

How to answer

Reply in chat with one pick per decision, plus any tweaks, e.g.

D1 C · D2 B · D3 A · D4 B · D5 B · D6 A+B · D7 B

The rec tag marks my recommendation for each. Colors can change at any point, but changing a decision after it's built means rewriting code.
