Air75 V2 – lighting drafts

Ten lighting schemes for your keymap, each running live on your exact layout and cap positions. The mockups use the same math the firmware would (ripple falloff, splash rings, the two top rails beside the Esc keys – one light each), with simulated typing so you can see each one react.

Schemes come in two kinds. A base is what the board does by default, and only one can be on at a time. An overlay sits on top of whatever base is on: it either turns on by itself in a certain situation, or you toggle it with an Fn key, like Fn + \ today.
